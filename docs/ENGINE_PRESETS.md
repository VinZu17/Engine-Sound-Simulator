# Engine Presets Reference

The simulator includes six engine presets. Each preset combines a different cylinder layout, redline, and sound character with its own torque configuration.

| Engine | Type | Cylinders | Redline | Character |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Lexus LFA V10 | V10 | 10 | 9,000 RPM | High-pitch scream |
| Subaru EJ25 | Boxer-4 | 4 | 7,000 RPM | Unequal-length header rumble |
| Toyota 2JZ-GTE | Inline-6 | 6 | 7,800 RPM | Smooth & clean |
| Honda F20C | Inline-4 | 4 | 9,000 RPM | VTEC crossover character |
| BMW M52B28 | Inline-6 | 6 | 6,500 RPM | Smooth & refined |
| Ferrari F136 V8 | Crossplane V8 | 8 | 9,000 RPM | Aggressive crossplane |

## Choosing a Preset

- Use the **Lexus LFA V10**, **Honda F20C**, or **Ferrari F136 V8** presets when exploring higher-revving engine behavior.
- Use the **BMW M52B28** preset for a lower redline and smoother character.
- The **Subaru EJ25** and **Toyota 2JZ-GTE** presets provide different four- and six-cylinder configurations for comparison.

These descriptions are intended as a quick reference; the simulator's actual behavior is determined by the engine configuration and physics/audio systems in the source code.
